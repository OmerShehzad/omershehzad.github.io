StudySync — Intelligent Academic Management and Student Productivity System

Project overview

StudySync consolidates academic planning, coursework, attendance, grades and study productivity into a personalised student workspace.

Technology stack

KotlinJetpack ComposeMaterial 3RoomSQLiteGradleCoroutinesAndroid SDK

Implementation and methodology

Iterative development combines a ViewModel and repository for core academic records with supplementary SQLite storage. Credit-weighted GPA, attendance calculations and deadline-first study allocation support academic decisions.

Key system features

Academic planning

Courses, assignments, examinations and weekly schedules.

Academic performance

Attendance, absence allowance, GPA and grade projections.

Productivity

Focus timer, study history, notes and goals.

Analytics and reporting

Academic summaries, charts and shareable PDF reports.

Account management

Local account separation and salted password hashes.

Data management

Personal records, fictional demonstration data and supplementary JSON backup.

Evaluation and limitations

Physical-device screenshots demonstrate the interface. Comprehensive acceptance testing remains unverified; backup covers supplementary records rather than full account recovery.

Functional documentation — 18 modules

StudySync combines academic administration and personal productivity in a native Android workspace. The following functional areas describe the application beyond the interfaces represented in the screenshot gallery.

01. Onboarding and authentication

Welcome and registration flows introduce the application; returning users can sign in to their local accounts.

02. Student profile and academic workspace

Provides a personalised workspace for organising student information and academic records.

03. Dashboard

Brings academic progress, upcoming commitments and key performance indicators into one overview.

04. Course management

Organises course records and provides the academic context for planning and performance tracking.

05. Timetable and university week

Organises recurring academic commitments and weekly schedules.

06. Assignment management

Tracks coursework and deadlines to support prioritisation.

07. Examination management

Organises examination commitments and preparation deadlines.

08. Attendance management

Tracks attendance, absence allowance and attendance risk to support academic decisions.

09. Grades and GPA simulator

Calculates credit-weighted GPA and supports grade projections through the simulator.

10. Focus timer and study history

Supports timed study sessions and review of recorded study activity.

11. Notes

Provides a dedicated space for academic notes.

12. Goals

Organises personal academic and productivity objectives.

13. Study planning

Uses deadline-first allocation to support prioritisation of study activities.

14. Reminders

Provides a dedicated interface for managing academic reminders.

15. Academic analytics

Presents academic summaries and charts, including attendance and grade information.

16. Reporting and PDF export

Provides academic reports and shareable PDF output.

17. Backup and restore

Supports supplementary JSON backup; this is not a full account recovery mechanism.

18. Settings and developer information

Provides access to application configuration and developer information.

Academic decision support

Credit-weighted GPA, attendance calculations and deadline-first study allocation provide practical decision support. These are calculation and scheduling features, not claims of a trained machine learning model.

Architecture and data management

The Kotlin and Jetpack Compose presentation layer uses Material 3 components. A ViewModel and repository coordinate core academic records, with Room and SQLite providing local persistence and supplementary SQLite storage supporting additional records. Local accounts use salted password hashes and separate personal records.

Development and validation

The application was run on a Samsung Galaxy S22 Ultra. The GitHub Actions workflow successfully completed the Android debug build and debug unit-test tasks. This does not establish comprehensive acceptance testing or production readiness.
